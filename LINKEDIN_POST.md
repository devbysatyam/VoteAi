# 🚀 Exciting News: Introducing VoteAI 🗳️

I am thrilled to announce the launch of **VoteAI**, a production-grade Election Assistant designed to empower every Indian voter with technology! 🇮🇳

Voting is the heartbeat of our democracy, but the process can sometimes feel overwhelming. I built VoteAI to simplify that journey—from understanding eligibility to finding your exact polling booth.

### 🌟 Key Features:
- **🤖 Smart AI Assistant**: Powered by **Google Gemini 1.5**, getting instant answers to any election-related query is now as easy as chatting.
- **🗺️ Dynamic Booth Finder**: Real-time Google Maps integration that geocodes your location to find the nearest polling station.
- **🪪 Digital Voter ID Card**: A personalized, sharable digital card to track your "Readiness Score."
- **🎮 Gamified Journey**: A step-by-step interactive guide (Eligibility → Documents → EVM Demo → Rights) that rewards you with badges as you prepare to vote.
- **🔒 Secure & Private**: Built with Firebase Auth and Firestore, ensuring your data is yours alone.

### 🛠️ The Tech Stack:
- **Frontend**: React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Zustand
- **AI**: Google Generative AI (Gemini SDK)
- **Maps**: Google Maps JavaScript API & Geocoding
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express, Firebase
- **Testing**: Vitest (80+ Unit & Integration Tests)
- **Deployment**: Vercel

This project was a deep dive into building highly accessible, performant, and "wow" UX for a social cause. I'm proud of how it turned out!
